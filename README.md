# Dria App

Crossword Puzzle solving application.

Building a foundation of vocabulary in your target language, such that you can expand upon that foundation to acquire language.

We apply language learning and learning principles to help you actually acquire a language of your choice.
A new language learner cannot understand even the simplest sentences and therefore attempting to read in your target language would comprise of 0% known language, we attempt to develop a base foundation of words that you know and expand upon that foundation of knowledge by implementing 80/20 learning rules and learning based upon comprehensible context / input.
